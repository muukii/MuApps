## Description

**Character Limit: 4,000**

Tinycurve is a journaling app for keeping feelings, meaningful moments, and things you are looking forward to in the form that fits them.

Capture daily thoughts, photos, sounds, or hand-drawn sketches. Even when words are not enough, you can choose the form that feels right for the moment.

You do not need to summarize your whole day or write a long entry. Keep one thing at a time. As your entries gather, they reveal more of your days and the moments that matter to you.

Use Tinycurve on your own as a personal journal for reflecting on everyday experiences and feelings.

Use it with the people you care about as a shared place to keep memories. Save places you want to visit, things you want to try, and plans you are looking forward to—and imagine what comes next together.

Tinycurve is not only a place for looking back.

It is a place to reflect on who you are now, preserve memories with the people who matter, and look forward to what is ahead.

KEY FEATURES

• Give text, links, photos, audio, doodles, and geometric artwork a fitting presentation
• Keep one small, meaningful thing at a time
• Share a space with the people you care about
• Sync across your devices with Apple iCloud
• See the latest entry from a chosen space in a widget
• No ads, tracking, or developer-operated analytics
